title: The future of software engineering
---

The junior who pastes a prompt and accepts the first diff will lose to the engineer who can specify, verify, and defend.

I do not think "AI will replace engineers" is a useful sentence. I think the job is moving toward ownership of representation: what the system is, what the change means, what evidence would falsify the claim.

Hiring will lag that shift. Portfolios that are still galleries of cloned apps will keep failing. Portfolios that are records of inherited systems, failures, and defenses will start to look like the job.
