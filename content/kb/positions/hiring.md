title: Hiring

I hire, and I teach people who want to be hired, on the same rule: show me a URL I can check in under a minute.

Take-home clones and certificate walls do not do that. A live system, a failure log, a graph of a change, a recorded defense: those do.

I will not tell a student that a job is guaranteed. I will tell them whether the work in front of me would survive a skeptical engineer.
