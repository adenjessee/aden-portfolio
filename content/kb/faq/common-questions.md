Are you open to work? Am I available to hire? I am open to conversations about applied AI, agentic systems, developer tooling, and founding engineer roles. My location and availability notes have the details on remote work and start date. The fastest way to talk is to book a call or leave me a message through AI Aden.

Do you freelance or take contract work? My client work notes say where I stand on contracts right now. If you have a project in mind, leave me a message with a short description and I will reply myself.

Can I see your code? Yes. My GitHub is github.com/adenjessee. GridSeak, my code graph tool, is at github.com/adenjessee/gridseak, and the source for this portfolio and AI Aden is at github.com/adenjessee/aden-portfolio. Most GridSchool code is private because it handles student data.

What is GridSchool's price? How much does GridSchool cost? The GridSchool price is quoted on a call. It is not published on this site or by AI Aden. You can book a call from gridschool.org.

Do you guarantee a job? Does GridSchool guarantee placement? No. GridSchool does not promise a job or a placement rate. A company's yes is still theirs. What GridSchool does promise is review of every step until a company hires you, and a week-2 exit: if either side says wrong room by the end of week 2, the unused tuition comes back.

Is this really Aden? Am I talking to a real person? AI Aden is an AI. It answers in my voice, only from notes I wrote on this site, and it cites the notes behind every answer. It can be wrong, and if no note covers your question it says I have not written a position. For anything that needs my judgment or a commitment, leave me a message or book a call and I will answer myself.

What model powers AI Aden? What AI does this site use? AI Aden runs on Anthropic's Claude by default, and I can swap the model through configuration. Retrieval combines BM25 keyword search with Cloudflare Workers AI embeddings. The model fills a fixed schema and never writes code. Each answer shows its sources and what it cost. The public evals page shows how it performs.

Why only five questions? Each visit gets five live questions to keep model cost bounded under a daily dollar ceiling. After that it keeps answering for free in notes-only mode, which assembles answers straight from my notes without a model. Every page also works as plain HTML.

How do I contact you? Leave a message through AI Aden, book a call through the site, or find me on GitHub at github.com/adenjessee and on LinkedIn as Aden Jessee.

Where are you based? I live in Boise, Idaho, and work on Mountain time.
