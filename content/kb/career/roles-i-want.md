title: Roles I want
---

The roles I am looking for are applied AI engineer, agentic systems engineer, developer tooling engineer, and founding engineer at an early company. The common thread is building AI systems whose output a team can check before trusting it.

As an applied AI engineer, I want to work on the part of the product where a model meets real data and real users. That means retrieval, tool calling, structured output, evals, and cost controls. I have built all of those in production at GridSchool and on this site.

As an agentic systems or developer tooling engineer, the work I am best at is giving agents a structural view of a codebase. GridSeak is my example: a deterministic graph of a repository that tells an agent what a change will touch, with the evidence tier for each claim. Teams building coding agents, code review tools, or internal developer platforms are a natural fit.

As a founding engineer, I bring the habits of someone who has run a whole product alone. I can take a vague problem, ship a first version, talk to the users, and keep the costs bounded. I am comfortable owning a system end to end.

The kind of team I want is one that reviews code seriously, writes things down, and cares whether the AI output is correct as much as whether it demos well. I do my best work where engineers are trusted to own a problem and expected to explain their changes. Team size matters less to me than that culture.

What I am less suited for is a role that is only prompt writing with no access to the system around it, or a team that measures AI work by volume of generated code. [MOCK: any roles Aden would rule out, e.g. management-only roles, specific industries].
