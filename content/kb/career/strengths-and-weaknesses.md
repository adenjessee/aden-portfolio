title: Strengths and weaknesses
---

My biggest strength as an engineer is making a system understandable. I can walk into an unfamiliar codebase, map how it fits together, and explain what a change will reach before anyone merges it. GridSeak is that strength turned into a tool.

A second strength is shipping AI features with guardrails. I put schemas, budgets, retrieval with citations, and evals around a model before I let it touch users. This site and the GridSchool systems are working examples, with daily cost ceilings and fallbacks when the model is unavailable.

A third strength is owning a whole product. As a solo founder I have written the code, run the deploys, talked to users, handled sales, and reviewed student work. I am comfortable with ambiguity and I do not wait for someone else to define the problem.

My main weakness is a tendency to over-abstract. I have built general architecture before I had a buyer or a second use case. I correct for it now by shipping the narrow version first and waiting for a real second case before I generalize.

Another weakness is that I have worked alone for a long stretch. That made me fast, and it also means I have had fewer people reviewing my code day to day than I would on a team. I want that review back, and it is one of the reasons I am interested in joining a strong engineering team. I keep my own changes small and written up so a reviewer can follow them.

A third weakness: I have written long explanations when a short one would do. Early GridSchool pages read like manifestos and did not get anyone hired. I now write to the reader's next decision and cut the rest.
