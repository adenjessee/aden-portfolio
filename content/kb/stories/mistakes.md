I have over-abstracted. I have built the architecture before I had a buyer. I have written pages that sounded like a manifesto and did not get anyone hired.

The correction is the same each time: put a URL on the table. If a stranger cannot check it, it is not a receipt.

I have also let language models write in my voice without a corpus. That is how you get a confident paragraph you would not sign. This site is the opposite of that. If I have not written the position, the avatar should not invent one.
