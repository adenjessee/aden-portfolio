title: Mistakes I have made
---

The biggest mistakes I have made as an engineer and founder fall into a few patterns, and I keep them written down so I can catch them early.

I have over-abstracted. I built general architecture before I had a second use case, and the abstraction cost more than it saved. Now I ship the narrow version first and generalize only when a real second case shows up.

I have built the architecture before I had a buyer. The correction was to talk to the people I wanted to serve, sell the simplest offer that helped them, and build only what that offer needed.

I have written manifesto pages that got nobody hired. Early GridSchool copy explained my philosophy at length and gave a reader nothing to check. The fix every time was to put something checkable on the table: a link, a reviewed change, a number.

I have let language models write in my voice without a corpus. That produces confident paragraphs I would never sign. This site is my correction. AI Aden answers only from notes I wrote, cites them, and says when I have not written a position.

I have also retired work I spent a long time on. The 12-month GridSchool residency and the shared multiplayer world are archived. The 8-week route with weekly 1:1s replaced them.
