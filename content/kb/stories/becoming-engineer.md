title: Becoming an engineer
---

I did not arrive at graph engineering from a research lab. I arrived from shipping software and then having to explain, later, why a change in one file broke something three folders away.

The first time you inherit a system you did not design, the tutorial portfolio becomes obviously fake. That is the moment I still design for. Can you say what you touched, what else it reaches, and how you would know if you were wrong?
