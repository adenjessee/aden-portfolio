title: Building GridSchool

GridSchool started because I was tired of watching people who could write a program fail a hiring loop that asked for proof they had never been taught to produce.

I tried several offer shapes. The one that survived is a residency, not a course. The student works every day. I review work that names a doubt. The map only lights a URL. I stay reachable after a hire.

The school is still being tuned on the founding students. That is stated in the offer. I will not pretend a brand-new method is a finished institution.
