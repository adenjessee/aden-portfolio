title: Hire job-search engine
---

The Hire engine is the job-search system I built for GridSchool students. Its job is to take the busywork out of a job search so the student's time goes into the parts only they can do: preparing, talking to people, and doing the work.

Each GridSchool student gets a daily run from the Hire engine, ordered by evidence. The order is prep first, then insider referrals found in the student's own LinkedIn connections export, then profile work, warm asks, follow-ups, prepared fresh roles, and finally the next step on their map. The most useful action comes first each day.

The Hire engine prepares specific applications with role packs. A role pack gathers what a student needs for one application: the role, the fit against their work, and the material to send. A role matcher ranks roles against the student's goal and evidence, and a radar feed watches the Hacker News "Who's hiring" threads for new roles.

Apply Assist is a browser extension that is part of the Hire system. It fills in job applications from the student's prepared material, and it never submits on its own. The student reviews and clicks submit every time.

The Hire engine has one hard rule: nothing automates a student's LinkedIn. It reads a connections export the student downloads themselves, and every message to a real person is sent by the student. Automating LinkedIn would put students' accounts at risk and would turn warm intros into spam.

A resume drafter and an admin desk sit alongside the Hire engine. The drafter produces a first resume from the student's actual work, and I review it. The admin desk is where I review student runs, role packs, and drafts before they matter.

The Hire engine is applied AI work in the plain sense. Models draft and rank, deterministic code orders and checks, and a human approves anything that leaves the system.
