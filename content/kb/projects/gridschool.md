GridSchool is the engineering residency I run at gridschool.org through my company, GRID SCHOOL LLC. The promise on the site is "Your next engineering role, mapped from where you stand." Each student gets a route built around their goal, and a working engineer, me, reviews every step until a company hires them.

The GridSchool residency format is 8 weeks of teaching and review at 10 to 15 hours a week. After week 8, the weekly 1:1 continues until a company hires the student. Each week includes a 45-minute 1:1, up to three written reviews, a 75-minute cohort call with up to five people, and same-day answers in Discord. Every written review comes back accepted or sent back with the one thing to change first.

GridSchool is for three kinds of engineer. The first is new to the field: a CS grad, bootcamp grad, or self-taught developer who has sent hundreds of applications and has never had a team see them own a change. The second is working but invisible outside their team, often because their best work sits behind an NDA. The third is between roles or laid off, and needs applications and warm intros moving in week one while one sharp project runs alongside.

A GridSchool student leaves with four things. A reviewed change to a real system, with the pull request, the review thread, and the check that catches the defect. A written verdict from an engineer who did not help them, filled in live at the defense. A case study page on their own domain covering the problem, the baseline, what changed, and what the numbers cannot prove. A recorded defense judged by an outside engineer, where they explain their strongest work and then handle a change they did not see coming.

GridSchool pricing is quoted on the call. The founding terms rise once the founding five students have defended in front of outside engineers. If either side decides by the end of week 2 that it is the wrong room, the unused tuition comes back. The one condition is to work every day. If a student goes quiet for three weeks, the spot pauses and I call them.

GridSchool does not promise a job. A company's yes is still theirs. The residency is not for people who cannot yet build something end to end, people who want a job-or-refund promise, or people who will not have their work reviewed.

Earlier versions of GridSchool were a 12-month residency with a fixed curriculum and a shared multiplayer world. Both are retired. The current residency is the 8-week route described here.
