GridSchool is a 12-month engineering residency. The buyer is a graduate who can code and cannot get hired.

The live product is at gridschool.org. The method is an evidence curriculum: comprehend, communicate, verify, vision. Students ship on a live multiplayer world, build their own system, learn graph engineering, publish a site and LinkedIn that match the work, and defend it.

The founding cohort is priced to fill seats while I still do the 1:1s myself. Later pricing is higher once defenses exist that outside readers have scored.
