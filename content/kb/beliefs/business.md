I sell work I can stand next to. GridSchool's founding price is low because the asset is me, directly, and the method is still being tuned on the first students.

I do not sell job-or-refund. That promise trains both sides to lie.

Cost is part of the design. A viral demo that bankrupts the server is not impressive. Rate limits, daily ceilings, cached paths, and a site that still works when the model is off are product decisions, not afterthoughts.
