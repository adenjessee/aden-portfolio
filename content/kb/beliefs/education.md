Most "learn to code" products sell activity. Activity is not evidence.

GridSchool is built around a different scoreboard. A node lights when a URL exists. A student leaves with a merged change to a live system, a personal site, a public trail, and a defense in front of an engineer who did not help them.

I will not write a student's production code. I will sit next to the work, name the next action, and refuse a review that does not name a doubt.

I do not promise a job. I promise the conditions under which a hire becomes possible: work that can be checked, a story that matches the work, and practice defending it.
