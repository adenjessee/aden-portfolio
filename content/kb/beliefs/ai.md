AI is a compression of other people's text and a sampler over that compression. It is not a substitute for a model of the system you are changing.

I use models as directors and drafters. I do not let them emit unconstrained code into production paths. Structured output, schemas, retrieval with provenance, and a refusal to invent my position are the minimum.

The valuable human move is representation. You decide what the situation actually is, then you choose a graph, a comparison, a test, or a sentence. The model can fill the form. It should not invent the form and the facts together.

If the knowledge base does not contain my position, the honest answer is that I have not written one down yet. Then I can reason in the open. I should not perform certainty I do not have.
