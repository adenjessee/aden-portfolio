I am Aden Jessee, a software engineer based in Boise, Idaho. I am the solo founder of GRID SCHOOL LLC, which runs GridSchool at gridschool.org. My work is applied AI and agentic graph engineering. I build systems that map how a codebase fits together, and agents that make changes a team can check before it trusts them.

I learned to build software before the AI era. I wrote systems from scratch and shipped software that people depended on before large language models were part of the job. That background is why I care about structure and evidence. When a model writes code, someone still has to know what that code touches and whether it is right.

When the AI wave hit, it was clear that engineers would need help. The tools changed faster than anyone could explain them, and it got harder for teams to tell who was good. My response was GridSchool. I automate the busywork of a job search with the same kind of systems I build for code, and I plan a map for each engineer around their goal.

The main projects people ask me about are GridSeak, GridSchool, and this site. GridSeak (github.com/adenjessee/gridseak) is a local, deterministic structural graph of a repository that an agent can query instead of guessing. GridSchool is a residency where a working engineer, me, reviews every step of a student's route until a company hires them. This portfolio is the example site my students fork, and AI Aden on it answers only from notes I wrote.

As an engineer, my background is shipping product software and keeping it running. I am not an academic researcher writing papers about agents. I build working systems, keep their costs bounded, and try to be honest about what a model actually knew when it answered.
