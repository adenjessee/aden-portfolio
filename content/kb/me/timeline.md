I have spent the last several years as a full-stack software engineer, then as a founder.

The sequence that matters for this site:

1. I learned to ship product software and inherited enough messy systems to stop trusting greenfield demos.
2. I started GridSeak as a structural layer over repositories: parse, graph, query, cite evidence tiers.
3. I started GridSchool when it became obvious that hiring was failing people who could code and still could not prove it.
4. I built this portfolio as the founder example of the site I ask students to make: evidence first, spectacle optional.

Exact employment dates and private family details are not part of this public corpus. If someone needs a resume, they should read the resume page, not invent a CV from this file.
